The English records in `official-first-batch.xml` preserve unmodified metadata
field values generated from the ArduPilot source annotations. ArduPilot and its
contributors retain their copyrights. The source project is licensed under
GPL-3.0-or-later; see the repository's [COPYING.txt](../../COPYING.txt) and
[ArduPilot's license](https://github.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot/blob/master/COPYING.txt).

The source registry records pinned upstream XML URLs for supplied metadata and
immutable ArduPilot source archives for historical reconstructions. Its PDEF
digest identifies the original complete file, with separate source archive
digests and tag or board evidence for reconstructions. Indentation and bundle
wrappers are local packaging. Most official inputs are included as selected
records. Reconstructed inputs include their complete parameter trees.

The official 4.5.7 files identify their tags; the corresponding commits were
resolved through the ArduPilot GitHub tag refs.

The independent Chinese notes in
`../zh-Hans/parameter-translations.json` and its generated `zh-CN.xml` are also
distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later. They are explanatory translations of the pinned
metadata, not additional flight-control rules or evidence of flight validation.
